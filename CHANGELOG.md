# Changelog

## v1.1
- Play vs computer with Stockfish and an adjustable Elo (800 to Max), undo and engine hints.
- Adaptive puzzles: 275 Stockfish-checked puzzles with a rating that adapts as you solve.
- Blunder detective: import a chess.com game (link plus username) or paste a PGN; Stockfish labels inaccuracies, mistakes
  and blunders and shows the better move with an explanation.
- Packaged for Windows (installer), Android (APK) and iPhone (home-screen web app), all offline.
- Slower, animated piece movement; move explanations; arrows in Learn mode.
- Added MIT license, third-party notices and a full README.
